## What the public page is

Every broker workspace on Majarrah gets a **public page** at:

```
https://www.majarrah.io/broker/<your-handle>
```

It's your public face on the marketplace: bio, verified badge, current listings, viewing hours, WhatsApp CTA. Buyers can browse everything you've published without an account.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Public page">
    From your dashboard sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your handle">
    Short and memorable. Once set, it lives in the URL — changing it later breaks any inbound links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your bio">
    A short pitch in both Arabic and English. Think 2-3 sentences: who you are, what areas you cover, what makes you different.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your logo">
    Square, transparent background, at least 256×256px.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Toggle the **Published** switch. Your page goes live immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track visits

Under **Settings → Public page → Analytics** you'll see:

- Total page views
- WhatsApp CTA clicks (a key intent signal)
- Traffic sources (where the visits came from)

The WhatsApp click count is also broken down per source so you can see if a listing on Snapchat is actually driving conversations.

## Share it

Once published, share your handle everywhere: your bio on Snapchat, Instagram, X, WhatsApp, business cards. The page is designed to convert: whether the visitor knows Majarrah or not, they can browse and message you in seconds.

<Note>
  Your public page only shows listings that are both **Published** and **Moderation-approved**. Pending or rejected listings are hidden.
</Note>
