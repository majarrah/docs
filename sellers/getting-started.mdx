## Create your account

Go to [app.majarrah.io](https://app.majarrah.io), sign up, and select **Seller** as your role during onboarding.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Add your full name and phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your property">
    Go to **Properties** in your dashboard and add your first listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Your listing is reviewed by Majarrah before going live. This typically takes 1 business day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive inquiries">
    Once published, buyers can contact you directly through your listing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your dashboard

As a seller, your dashboard gives you access to:

| Section | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| Properties | Add and manage your listings |
| Inquiries | View and respond to buyer questions |
| Bookings | Manage scheduled property viewings |
| WhatsApp | Get inquiry notifications on WhatsApp |
| Settings | Manage your profile |

<Note>
  Sellers list individual properties. If you're a licensed real estate broker managing multiple clients and listings, see the [Broker guide](/brokers/getting-started) instead.
</Note>
