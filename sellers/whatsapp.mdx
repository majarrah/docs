---
title: "WhatsApp Notifications"
description: "Get instant alerts on WhatsApp when buyers contact you."
---

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open WhatsApp settings">
    Go to **WhatsApp** from your dashboard sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your number">
    Select your country code and enter your WhatsApp number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Enter the verification code sent to your WhatsApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable notifications">
    Toggle notifications on. You'll be alerted instantly when a buyer sends an inquiry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disabling or changing your number

Toggle the switch off on the WhatsApp page to pause notifications. To change your number, disable the current one and set up a new one.
